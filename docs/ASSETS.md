## Card illustrations (`assets/cards/`)

**Deck:** Rider–Waite–Smith tarot, illustrated by **Pamela Colman Smith**, first published in 1909 by
William Rider & Son, London.

**License:** public domain. The artist died in 1951, so the artwork is out of copyright in
countries with life + 70 years or shorter terms. Under US law it was published in 1909, before 1930.
Wikimedia Commons hosts the scans as public domain. Use only these original 1909 scans. Later
recoloured editions (for example the 1971 U.S. Games edition) are still under copyright and must
not be used.

**Pipeline:** `npm run images -- --download` saves each original to `assets/cards/source/` (not
committed) from `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/<file>`. It then writes a
300 px and a 900 px WebP per card to `assets/cards/thumb/` and `assets/cards/full/`, and regenerates
`src/data/cardImages.ts`.

**Status:** not downloaded yet. The build sandbox's network policy blocked `commons.wikimedia.org`
and `upload.wikimedia.org`. Until the images are added, the app shows typographic card faces.
After downloading, check each file page below to confirm it is the 1909 scan, and update this
section.
