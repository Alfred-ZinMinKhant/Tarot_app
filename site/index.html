What is Mingalar Tarot?

Mingalar Tarot is a free tarot reading app for Android, made for readers who want tarot in both English and Burmese. Each of the 78 cards of the Rider–Waite–Smith deck, from the Major Arcana to the four suits of the Minor Arcana, has an upright and reversed meaning you can read in either language.

Daily card, spreads and journal

Start the day with your Card of the Day, or draw a one-card or three-card spread when you have a question. Save your thoughts about each draw in a private journal that is stored on your own phone. The app works offline.
