Delete your Mingalar Tarot account

This page explains how to delete the optional cloud backup account for the Mingalar Tarot app (developer: Zin Min Khant). You only have an account if you signed in with Google to back up your journal. If you never signed in, nothing is stored on our servers.

Steps in the app

  1. Open Mingalar Tarot.
  2. Go to Settings → Cloud backup.
  3. Tap Delete cloud account and confirm.

Can't open the app?

Email zinminkhant.alfred@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete my Mingalar Tarot account". We will delete your account and data and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including the email address of your Google account.
  • Your cloud backup: readings, questions, notes, Card of the Day history, settings and birth chart details (birth date, optional time and place).

What is kept

  • Nothing about you is kept on our servers after deletion.
  • Data on your phone stays there until you use Settings → Delete my data or uninstall the app.
  • Google may keep data related to ads and to your Google account under its own policies.
